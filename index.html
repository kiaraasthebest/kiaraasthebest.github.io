About us

Our story

The Present Tense was started by two friends in Windhoek, Namibia, who believe that giving shouldn't stop when the season ends.

We're a youth- and women-led initiative that makes celebrations possible and provides essential supplies, all year round. Our name is a promise: we show up in the present, for every occasion.

Any funds we don't need to run our projects will be donated to charities, including those supporting people affected by gender-based violence.

FAQ

How does donating work?

You can give through our GoFundMe, which handles payments securely, or drop gifts off once our giving trees open. We'll explain every step here.

Where do the gifts go?

Gifts go to individuals, families and community groups in Namibia who need them. We'll share where each campaign's gifts end up.

How are recipients chosen?

We're working with schools, community groups and other trusted organisations so that gifts reach the people who need them most. We'll explain the process here once it's final.

How can I help?

You can volunteer, run a collection at your school or church, start a club or sponsor a campaign. Visit our Get Involved page or send us a message.

Can I volunteer if I'm under 18?

Yes, with a parent or guardian's permission. Send us a message and we'll tell you what to do next.

Will you share photos of children?

No identifiable photos of children are published without consent from a parent or guardian.

When does the Angel Tree open?

We don't have a date yet. Add your email on the Angel Tree page and we'll tell you as soon as children's submissions open.

Safeguarding and privacy

How we protect children

The safety and dignity of every child and family we support comes first. Children's details are only collected and used for the purpose of giving them a gift, and are shared only with the people who need them to do that.

Photos

We never publish identifiable photos of children without consent from a parent or guardian.

Young volunteers

Volunteers under 18 need a parent or guardian's permission before they take part.

How we use your information

When you sign up to our email list or send us a message, we ask for your name and email address. We use them only to reply to you and to tell you about our projects. We won't sell your details, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.
